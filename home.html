Likhitha Chintareddy is a user experience researcher, software engineer, and a graduate student in public policy at the University of Chicago.

Her work focuses on using human-computer interaction methods to examine technologies that are designed to shape behavior at a societal scale.

She earned her B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University.
